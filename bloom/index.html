Bloom: Petal Chain

Chain-reaction garden strategy for iPhone.

Add petals to a hexagon garden. Fill a bed and it blooms, throwing petals into every bed around it and taking those flowers for you. One tap can set off a chain across the whole garden. Play the computer from Easy to Master, the 12-stage Garden Tour, or pass and play with up to 4 people on one phone.
